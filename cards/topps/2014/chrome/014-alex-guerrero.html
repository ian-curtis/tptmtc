The Play

February 26, 2014

Los Angeles Dodgers @ Arizona Diamondbacks

Bottom of the 3rd

Alex Guerrero turns a double play, throwing to first from second.

There is no visual evidence for this play. Guerrero did not play in 2013 so the play had to have taken place in 2014. The card shows the Dodgers' spring training jerseys, and shows Guerrero turning a double play (at second base even though his position on the card indicates shortstop). According to Statcast, there was only one double play in spring training that involved Guerrero and an opposing team wearing a red jersey and helmet.
