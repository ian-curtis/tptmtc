The Play

July 26, 2024

Atlanta Braves @ New York Mets

Top of the 5th

Nacho Alvarez Jr. hits the ball far to right field which is caught by Jeff McNeil.

The video of this play is found on the MLB Film Room. We can't see the exact position on the card in the video but this is the only play in 2024 where Alvarez Jr. is at Citi Field (can see bricks in background of card), is wearing a grey jersey and blue sleeves, is looking towards right field, and is wearing light grey shoes. The other choice for this play is July 28 of the same year; however, he is wearing dark grey cleats, at least that is what it visible in the video of his hit that day.
