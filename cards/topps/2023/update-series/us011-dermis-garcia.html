The Play

July 24, 2022

Texas Rangers @ Oakland Athletics

Bottom of the 9th

Garcia waits for a pitch.

A video of what could be this play can be found on the MLB Film Room. This is the only day game where Garcia wore a green jersey and white sleeve on his right arm. The official photo on Getty Images indicates this was from the final inning of the game (in fact it was the final at-bat of the game). The photo was edited to show Garcia with wearing number 40.

Fun fact: Garcia did not play in the MLB in 2023. He changed his jersey number on March 30, 2023 (Opening Day) and was DFA'd about a month later. At some point in this time frame is when Topps finalized the card list for the 2023 Update Series.
