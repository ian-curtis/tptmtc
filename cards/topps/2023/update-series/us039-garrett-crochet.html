The Play

September 12, 2021

Boston Red Sox @ Chicago White Sox

Unknown

Crochet throws a pitch.

A video of what could be this play can be found on the MLB Film Room. Crochet did not play in 2022 due to an injury and didn't play early enough in 2023 to have a photo for a card. Therefore, this photo came from 2021. Crochet had several sunny home games with an alternate jersey but the official photo on Getty Images indicates that this is the game.
