The Play

June 29, 2022

Wilson swings at a pitch on his MLB debut.

Evidence for this play can be found on The MLB Film Room. It is unclear which at bat resulted in the image on the card. However, this was the only game in which Wilson played with a dark blue jersey and dark blue sleeve (without stars).
