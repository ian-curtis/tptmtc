The Play

August 1, 2022

Arizona Diamondbacks @ Cleveland Guardians

Bottom of the 11th

The Guardians celebreate after Amed Rosario hits a walk-off single.

Evidence for this play can be found on News 5 Cleveland (Direct Image Link) It is also possible to see the play on the MLB Film Room; however the last second of the video is only the beginning of the moment pictured on the card.
