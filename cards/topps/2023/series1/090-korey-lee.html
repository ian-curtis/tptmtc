The Play

July 15, 2022

Oakland Athletics @ Houston Astros

Bottom of the 3rd

Lee grounds out.

Evidence for this play can be found on the MLB Film Room (at 0:04). This isn't other games where he wears the orange jersey as three things needed to be met: 1) white sleeve with blue on wrist, 2) letting go of the bat post-swing, and 3) black handled bat with light wood center on the knob.
