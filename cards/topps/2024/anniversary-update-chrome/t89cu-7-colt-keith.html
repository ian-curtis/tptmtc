The Play

March 28, 2024

Detroit Tigers @ Chicago White Sox

Top of the 7th

Colt Keith flies out to left field.

The video of this play is found on Statcast. Early March is the only time Keith wore blue sleeves on both arms and wore orange batting gloves at the same time. There is another flyout in this game that could easily have also been the one on the card but I am 90% confident the game date is correct.
